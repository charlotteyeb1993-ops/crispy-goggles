# Scene 3 — Foam Cannon Wash

## Purpose
Cover the luxury car in thick foam and create satisfying ASMR visuals.

## Shots
- Wide shot of the entire car being covered in foam
- Close-up of foam flowing over the hood
- Macro shot of foam collecting around the grille and badges
- Side shot showing foam running down the doors
- Close-up of foam dripping from the wheels

## Audio
- Foam cannon spray
- Thick foam bubbling
- Water dripping
- Soft ambient washing sounds

## Visual Style
Cinematic, slow camera movement, detailed macro shots, glossy highlights, relaxing ASMR atmosphere.
