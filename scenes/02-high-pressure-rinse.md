# Scene 02 — High-Pressure Rinse

## Purpose
Remove loose dirt and mud before the foam wash.

## Visuals
- Wide cinematic shot of the luxury car.
- Close-up of high-pressure water hitting the paint.
- Mud and grime washing away from the body panels.
- Detailed shots of wheels and wheel arches.

## ASMR
- High-pressure water spray.
- Water hitting metal and paint.
- Dirt and mud rinsing away.

## Camera
Slow tracking shots with macro close-ups of water, dirt, and paint.
