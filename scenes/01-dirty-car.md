# Scene 01 — Dirty Car Introduction

## Purpose
Establish the luxury car in its dirtiest condition before the wash begins.

## Shot
Slow cinematic wide tracking shot revealing the entire dirty vehicle.

## Detail Shots
- Mud in the wheel arches
- Dust across the paint
- Water spots on the glass
- Grime around badges and trim

## Audio
Natural outdoor ambience, subtle dirt and texture sounds, no music.

## Visual Style
Cinematic, realistic, high-detail, relaxing ASMR atmosphere.
