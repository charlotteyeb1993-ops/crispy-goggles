# Crispy Goggles — Luxury Car Wash ASMR

A cinematic car-washing project focused on luxury vehicles, satisfying cleaning scenes, and high-quality ASMR visuals.

## Project Overview

Crispy Goggles creates visually satisfying videos showing dirty luxury cars being professionally washed and restored to a clean, glossy finish.

The videos feature:

- 🚘 Luxury and exotic cars
- 💦 High-pressure water washing
- 🫧 Foam and soap application
- 🧽 Detailed hand washing
- ✨ Paint and chrome restoration
- 🎧 Relaxing ASMR-style cleaning sounds
- 🎥 Cinematic camera views
- 🔄 Before-and-after transformations

## Goal

Create relaxing and visually engaging car-washing videos that showcase the complete transformation from a dirty vehicle to a spotless, polished car.

## Video Sequence

1. Dirty Car Introduction
2. High-Pressure Rinse
3. Foam Cannon Wash
4. Detailed Hand Washing
5. Wheel and Tire Cleaning
6. Rinse and Water Beading
7. Drying and Polishing
8. Final Cinematic Reveal
